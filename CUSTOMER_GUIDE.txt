MR GROOM MANAGER — QUICK GUIDE

Logging in
  First time: username  admin   password  admin.
  Please press "Users" and change the admin password. The admin can also add staff users there.

Where is my data?
  In the folder Desktop\MrGroom .
  Data\     your live data file
  Backups\  automatic copies on this computer, every 10 minutes while you work, plus one per day

Google Drive backup (free, off your computer)
  After the first login the app asks for your Gmail. Press "Link Google Drive", sign in to Google in the
  browser with the SAME Gmail and press Allow. A backup is then sent to your Drive (folder "Mr GROOM Backups")
  every day while the app is open and the computer is online.
  To bring data back: Google Drive button -> Show backups -> Restore.

Every evening press "Close of Sales". It saves a local backup and sends one to Google Drive.
Optional: File -> "Choose extra local backup folder..." for a copy on a USB stick.

If someone deletes the MrGroom folder by mistake:
  Open the app. It offers to restore the latest backup. Click "Restore".
New computer: install, log in, then Google Drive -> Restore (or File -> Restore from a backup file).
Uninstalling the app does NOT delete your data.
